# Privacy Policy — Archipelago Notifier

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

## 1. Operator and Scope
Archipelago Notifier (the “Bot”) is an independent Discord bot that publishes notifications related to Archipelago sessions. The project is maintained by **Mineteck**: https://github.com/Mineteck/archipelago_notifier.

## 2. Data Processed
Depending on the features used, the Bot may process:
- Discord identifiers required to run commands and deliver notifications (server, channel, and, where needed, user identifiers);
- connection settings and notification-channel configuration;
- information received from configured Archipelago servers (session names, player or slot names, game events, items, hints, and other available notifications);
- technical information needed for troubleshooting and reconnection.

**Verify against the code and hosting environment before publishing:** exactly which identifiers are stored, log contents, whether room passwords or other secrets are saved, and whether the Bot reads Discord message content.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Basis
The information is used to connect requested sessions, configure and publish notifications to selected channels, operate and secure the Bot, and troubleshoot incidents.

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely, depending on the circumstances, on legitimate interests in providing and securing the service or on necessity to provide the requested functionality. The operator must confirm the appropriate legal basis for their actual use.

## 4. Storage and Retention
Settings may be retained while a configuration remains active. Temporary data is used while events are processed. Technical logs, backups, and leftover configuration data, if any, should be deleted according to a documented retention schedule.

**Complete before publishing:** storage location and method, exact retention periods for configurations and logs, and the deletion procedure after the Bot is removed or a room is deleted.

## 5. Recipients and Third-Party Services
Notifications are posted to configured Discord channels and may be visible to members according to server permissions. The Bot communicates with Discord and the Archipelago servers that users choose to connect to. The Bot's hosting provider may technically process data necessary to operate the service.

Data is not intended to be sold or used for advertising. Discord's and third-party Archipelago servers' own privacy policies also apply to their services.

## 6. Security
The operator aims to limit access to data and protect sensitive credentials and settings. No system can guarantee absolute security. Never share a Discord token or other secret in a public channel or GitHub issue.
